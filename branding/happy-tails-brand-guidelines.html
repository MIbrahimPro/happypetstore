01 / The story

THE SHOP THAT NEVER CLOSES.

Happy Tails began as a weekend counter in Ramna Plaza and grew into the shop Islamabad's pet owners call at 3 a.m. Three counters share one room: the store (food, litter, accessories, toys), the clinic (vaccines, medicines, the vet), and the front window (kittens and dogs, including rescues). The brand must always feel like that room: honest, stocked, awake.

HONEST

Real stock counts, real price bands, no inflated claims. Say "two left" when there are two.

AWAKE

24/7 is not a slogan, it is the operation. The brand speaks like someone on night duty.

HANDS-ON

Animals are held, not shipped. Advice is given over the counter, in plain language.

02 / Logo

THE RED MARK.

The logo is a red plate carrying a cream dog head and a curled tail stroke, with the wordmark set in a heavy grotesque. HAPPY in ink, TAILS in red. The mark is drawn from the shop's signboard: the leaping cat and dog against the sky.

HAPPYTAILS

DO

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the H on all sides.
  • Use the mark alone for avatars, stamps and favicons.
  • Minimum size: 24 px mark, 90 px horizontal lockup.

DON'T

  • Never add gradients, shadows, outlines or rounded corners.
  • Never recolour the dog or swap the red plate for another colour.
  • Never stretch, rotate or retype the wordmark in another font.

RATIOS

Paper and ink carry about 80 percent of any layout. Red is roughly 10 to 15 percent. Mustard, sage and steel share the rest. If a design looks "colourful", it is wrong.

04 / Typography

THREE FACES, NO MORE.

Display

Archivo Black

Headlines and buttons. Set in caps, tight leading, negative tracking. Never italic, never light.

Text

Archivo Regular

Body copy and product descriptions. Sentence case, generous line height.

Data

Space Mono

Prices, stock counts, labels, captions. Always uppercase with wide tracking.

05 / Graphic language

COUNTER-TOP GRAPHICS.

Devices borrowed from the physical shop. Use at least one per layout, never all at once.

2 px ink borders

Every box is drawn with a hard 2 px ink rule. Corners are always square.

Boxed content, 2 px rule

Offset hard shadow

Shadows are flat ink blocks, offset 4 px. No blur, ever.

4 px ink block

Dashed cut lines

Sections separate like coupon edges.

Sticker photos

Photos sit on white sticker mats, tilted at most 2 degrees.

Tape labels

Category tags are ink or red rectangles with mono caps, butted to the photo edge.

FOOD PHARMACY

Ruled and graph paper

Backgrounds may carry faint 28 px grid or receipt ruling. Text sits on plain zones.

06 / Voice

TALK LIKE THE COUNTER.

Plain, warm, exact. The brand speaks Urdu-hearted English the way Islamabad actually talks, without marketing varnish.

SAY

  • "Only two left, we restock Friday."
  • "Send the photo on WhatsApp, the vet will look first."
  • "Rs 1,400 a month for the spot-on course."
  • "Call before you drive over, we will keep it aside."

NEVER SAY

  • "Elevate your pet's lifestyle with premium solutions."
  • "Unleash happiness today!"
  • "Best quality guaranteed 100 percent."
  • Any exclamation mark. The brand does not use them.

MECHANICS

  • No em dashes. Use commas, colons, or full stops.
  • No exclamation marks anywhere in brand copy.
  • Numbers for everything countable: "2 kittens", "24/7", "Rs 6,900".
  • Urdu greetings welcome in scripts and captions: "Assalam o alaikum" opens WhatsApp orders.

07 / Photography

REAL ANIMALS, REAL SHELVES.

USE

  • Daylight photos of animals looking at the camera or mid-play.
  • Shelf photos that show real stock and hand-written price tags.
  • Warm tones that sit near Paper and Bone. Photos must not fight the palette.

AVOID

  • Studio-white pack shots floating on shadows.
  • Stock models holding puppies in cashmere.
  • Heavy filters, HDR halos, or any photo with a gradient wash.

Current site imagery is placeholder photography by Pexels and Pixabay photographers; replace with real shop photos as they are shot.
