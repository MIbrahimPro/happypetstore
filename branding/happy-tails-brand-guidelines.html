03 · The logo

Their own art, traced to SVG, backgrounds removed.

Wordmark construction

Happy

"Happy" set in Baloo 2 ExtraBold. "Tails" is the client's own script lettering, vectorized from the original art. Collar-red underline bar optional.

Clear space and minimums

Keep clear space equal to the height of the dog's ear on all sides. The mark never sits smaller than 28px tall on screens. The full lockup never under 140px wide. On photos, use the night sticker version or drop a soft shadow.

04 · Fur, swiggle, motion

Everything here has a coat.

Fur texture

Directional 1.5px strokes at 100°, two layers offset by half a cell, 4% opacity. Day fur on bone, night fur on night. It backgrounds sections; it never sits under body paragraphs busier than captions.

The swiggle

One hand-drawn line that leans toward the mouse and settles when it stops. Amber by default, turf for confirmations. It underlines headlines and section titles. One per viewport at rest; it is a wink, not a wallpaper.

Motion manners

  • Paw-print trail follows the cursor at a walking gait, fades in a second.
  • Cards lift 4px and tilt a third of a degree. No bouncing.
  • Photos sway ±1.2° over six seconds when idle. Nothing else breathes on a grid.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion: all of it switches off.

Never

  • No gradients, no glass blur panels, no neon glow.
  • No sharp corners. Radius lives at 1.5 to 2rem, pills at 999px.
  • No hard offset shadows; shadows are soft, night-tinted, low.
  • No amber text on bone. It fails at 1.9 and it always will.
